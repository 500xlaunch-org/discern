Privacy Policy

Xurface Discern, by 500xLaunch. Last updated 21 September 2026.

Xurface Discern exists so you can decide what an AI agent may do on your behalf. That job only works if the record of those decisions is trustworthy, so we keep the data we hold small, specific and yours.

The short version

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third party tracker in the app.
  • Notification content is encrypted end to end. The delivery service cannot read it.
  • You can delete your account and everything attached to it at any time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, health data, installed apps or an advertising identifier. The app requests no Android runtime permission other than notifications, which you grant explicitly and can revoke.

The audit record

Every action an agent takes through Xurface is written to a signed, hash chained ledger. Each entry carries the hash of the one before it, so an entry cannot be altered or removed without breaking the chain. This is what makes the record worth trusting. It also means entries are not individually editable: when you delete your account we delete the entries belonging to you rather than rewriting them in place.

Notifications

When an agent needs your judgement, the message is encrypted on our server to a key your own device generated, using the Web Push standard (RFC 8291). It travels through your browser or operating system vendor's push service, which can see that a message was sent to your device but cannot read what it says. Only your device can decrypt it.

Who else sees your data

A solution you connect learns only what it needs to act: that you exist as a pseudonymous reference, and the outcome of decisions you made about its own requested actions. It does not receive your email address unless it already knew it, and it never receives your decisions about other solutions.

We use Microsoft Azure to host the service, in the United States. We do not use any other processor for your data.

Your choices

  • See everything. The Activity screen shows every recorded action.
  • Stop an agent. Every connected solution has a kill switch that pauses all of its agents at once.
  • Turn notifications off. In the app's Settings, or in your device settings.
  • Delete your account. Write to the address below and we will delete your account and its records within 30 days.
  • Ask what we hold. We will send you a machine readable copy.

Children

Xurface Discern is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13, or under 16 where local law sets that age.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
