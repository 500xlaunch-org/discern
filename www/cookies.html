Cookies and storage

Xurface Horizon, Xurface Discern and Line, by 500xLaunch. Effective 3 October 2026.

The short version

  • None of these products sets a cookie.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no third party tracker, so nothing is shared with anyone for marketing.
  • What is kept in your browser or on your phone is only what the product needs to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your choices, your vault, and the app itself for when you are offline.
  • Because all of it is strictly necessary, there is nothing to switch off and no banner asking you to. Clearing your browser's site data, or signing out, removes it.

Exactly what is kept, and why

Everything below stays on your device. The vault is encrypted there with a key that never leaves it.

Passkeys

Signing in uses a passkey. It is kept by your device or your password manager, not by us and not in the browser storage above. We hold only its public half.

If this changes

If we ever add something that is not strictly necessary, we will ask you first, in the product, before it is set, and this page will say what it is. The terms you accept when you create an account include this notice, with its version, so a change is asked again.
