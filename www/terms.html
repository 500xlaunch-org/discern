1.What Xurface is

Xurface is a checkpoint between an AI agent's intention and its consequence. Developers register their agents and declare what those agents can do. Horizon scores each ability for risk. When an agent is about to do something that goes beyond what you have said it may do on its own, Horizon stops and asks you in the Discern app, and the agent waits for your answer. We built it to make AI safer for everybody, and we offer it on that basis.

5.The vault

The vault holds things an agent might need to reach on your behalf: a wifi password, a login, a one-time code seed, a card. It is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your PIN and, where your device supports it, its own hardware lock.

Nothing you put in the vault is ever stored on our servers. Not in the clear, not encrypted, not as a backup. We never have a copy, so we cannot read it, lose it, hand it over or restore it. The consequence is deliberate and you accept it by using the vault: if you forget your PIN and lose every device that holds your vault, its contents are gone, and nobody, including us, can recover them. After repeated wrong PINs the vault on that device erases itself.

6.Moving the vault between devices

You can copy your vault from one of your devices to another. To do it, the receiving device shows a short code and the sending device confirms the same code, which proves the two devices are talking to each other and not to anybody in between. The sending device then encrypts the vault for the receiving device alone.

That encrypted envelope passes through our servers because the two devices have no other way to reach each other. We cannot read it. It is held in memory only, never written to disk or to any backup, and it is deleted as soon as the receiving device collects it, or after ten minutes if it is not collected, whichever comes first. A transfer can be collected once. We keep a record that a transfer happened and when, never what it contained.

7.When an agent asks for something in the vault

An agent asking for something from your vault is always treated as a critical risk, whatever your other settings say. You decide each time whether to allow it:

  • once, for this request only; or
  • for the same purpose, so that the same agent asking for the same item for the same stated reason does not need to ask you again.

You can take back any allowance at any time in the app. If you change the value of an item, every allowance you gave for that item stops at once, and every agent has to ask you again. What you release is encrypted for the agent's Solution alone, passes through our servers only long enough to be collected once, and is not kept.
